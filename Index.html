About Me

I am an experienced biochemist who builds the reagents and laboratory systems that clinical testing depends on. My work covers diagnostic reagent development, clinical chemistry, product validation and the quality systems that keep every batch consistent.

From research and formulation to production and release, I focus on products that laboratories can trust. I also work on the operational side of diagnostics: laboratory management, inventory control and automation that reduce errors and turnaround time.

My goal is simple: bring scientific rigor and practical innovation together so healthcare teams get accurate results, faster.
